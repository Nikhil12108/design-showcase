Our approach

How we build

  1. I.

    Small on purpose.

    We never build more than twenty homes in one place. It keeps the scale human and the quality high.

  2. II.

    Materials that age well.

    Brick, timber, lime and flint. Things that look better in twenty years, not worse.

  3. III.

    The same team every time.

    One architect practice and two builders we have worked with for over a decade.
